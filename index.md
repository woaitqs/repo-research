Each project uses the same URL pattern:

- Research: `/research/<project-name>.html` (plus `<project-name>.zh.html` when a Chinese version exists)
- Diagrams: `/diagrams/<project-name>/<diagram>.html`, with a gallery at `/diagrams/<project-name>/`
- Experiment source: [`/experiments/<project-name>/`](https://github.com/woaitqs/repo-research/tree/main/experiments) (on GitHub; experiments are not published to Pages)

Studies distinguish **Fact** (read in source, tested, or observed at runtime) from
**Interpretation** (a reading of intent). Source references use `path:line` against the pinned commit.
