# open-pstack — interactive diagrams / 交互式图表

Eight [Archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) diagrams. Each node carries `SRC` badges
that link to the exact lines they were drawn from, all pinned to
[`ericlitman/open-pstack@b4c5e3d`](https://github.com/ericlitman/open-pstack/tree/b4c5e3d63318308b7e11f26b2bc156e7793d834a)
(Open Pstack 1.16.0).

8 张 [Archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) 图。每个节点都带有 `SRC` 标记，链接到绘制依据的具体源码行，
全部固定在 `ericlitman/open-pstack@b4c5e3d`（Open Pstack 1.16.0）。图中文字为英文，中英文文章共用同一套图。

<!-- diagram-cards -->

The diagram list (titles, types, questions, pins) lives in
[`_data/studies.yml`](https://github.com/woaitqs/repo-research/blob/main/_data/studies.yml).

All eight were finalized with `archify finalize --quality showcase --repo-root <clone at the pinned commit>`
(schema validation, verified delivery, strict provenance check, headless-browser check), and their
screenshots were reviewed by eye. The JSON specifications (Archify candidates), the gate results and a
regeneration recipe are in
[`assets/open-pstack/archify/`](https://github.com/woaitqs/repo-research/tree/main/assets/open-pstack/archify).

8 张图都通过了 `archify finalize --quality showcase --repo-root <固定版本的检出目录>`（schema 校验、可验证交付、
严格的来源检查、无界面浏览器检查），截图经人工查看。JSON 规格、各项检查结果与重新生成步骤见 `assets/open-pstack/archify/`（链接同上）。
